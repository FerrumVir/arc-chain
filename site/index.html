Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ARC Chain?
ARC is a decentralized AI inference network. Instead of relying on centralized cloud providers, ARC distributes AI workloads across a global network of community-run nodes. Every node contributes compute power and earns ARC tokens in return. The network uses GPU-accelerated BLAKE3 hashing for consensus and supports models like Llama 2 7B.
How do I earn ARC tokens?
Once your node is running, it automatically connects to the network and begins processing inference requests. You earn 100 ARC tokens per completed inference. Your earnings are persisted to disk, so you never lose progress even if your machine restarts. Track your earnings at http://localhost:9090.
Is it safe to run on my computer?
Yes. The ARC node runs a sandboxed AI model locally on your machine. It does not access your files, passwords, or personal data. By default, it uses only 15% of your CPU so you can use your computer normally while it runs in the background. The installer is open source and auditable on GitHub. On Mac, it runs as a launchd service; on Linux, as a systemd service.
How much can I earn?
Earnings depend on your hardware and uptime. Every completed inference earns 100 ARC. A typical Mac with an M-series chip completes an inference in about 1 second, meaning you can earn thousands of ARC per hour. Check the leaderboard above to see what top earners are making. The more powerful your hardware and the longer you run, the more you earn.
What hardware do I need?
Minimum: 8 GB RAM and 5 GB free disk space. The node runs a quantized 7B parameter model (4.1 GB). Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), Linux (x86_64 or ARM64), and Windows are all supported. Apple Silicon Macs, machines with NVIDIA GPUs, and high-core-count servers will earn faster, but any modern computer can participate.
How do I stop or uninstall?
On Mac: launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.arc-chain.node.plist. On Linux: sudo systemctl stop arc-node. To fully uninstall, just remove the ~/.arc directory. Your earnings history is stored there, so back it up first if you want to keep it.
